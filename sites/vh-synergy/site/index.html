Questions

What owners ask us

Do we have to take both services?

No. Many clients start with one service, such as ads or a growth plan, and add others later. The free audit tells you where to start.

How soon will we see results?

Ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. SEO and social media usually take three to six months to build. Consultancy work shows up as decisions and changes within the first month.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Yes. Plans are sized to the business, from a single shop or a new startup to companies with several locations.

Do you only work with businesses in Solapur?

No. Our office is in Solapur, Maharashtra, and we meet local clients in person. Most of the work happens online, so we also work with businesses in other cities.

What does business consultancy actually include?

Strategy and growth planning, startup guidance, market research, operations, budgeting and sales. You get written recommendations and help putting them into practice.

Do we need a long contract?

No. Monthly plans can stop with 30 days notice, and project work is quoted up front. Accounts, content and designs we create stay yours.
