Questions schools ask

How long until we see results?

Technical fixes often show movement within one to two months. Rankings for competitive course terms usually take three to six months. We tell you which is which before we start, so nobody is surprised.

Do you work with our course platform?

We work inside Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi, LearnWorlds, Podia, Moodle and WordPress with LearnDash or Tutor LMS. If yours is not listed, ask us. Most platforms allow the changes that matter.

We are a single course creator. Is this for us?

Yes. A small catalogue is often the fastest to fix, because every page can get full attention. The one-off audit is a good place to start.

Who writes the content?

We do. Your teachers review every page for accuracy before it is published, because students can tell when a course page was written by someone who has never taught.

What about AI search?

AI assistants and AI overviews pull from pages they can read and trust. We structure your course pages and answers so they can be quoted and linked, alongside classic rankings.

Can we stop at any time?

Yes. The Growth plan runs month to month with 30 days notice, and everything we write or fix stays yours.
